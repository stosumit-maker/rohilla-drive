## External/local signals still required
Search rankings cannot be guaranteed by code. Local pack visibility depends heavily on a complete Google Business Profile, correct business name/address/phone consistency, suitable primary/secondary categories, genuine customer reviews, current photos, local citations and real engagement. Do not fabricate reviews or locations.

## Content discipline
Do not create dozens of thin city/keyword doorway pages. Add location pages only when the business genuinely serves that area and the page has unique inventory, service or operational value.
