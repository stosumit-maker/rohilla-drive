# Ambala local SEO operating checklist

- Keep Google Business Profile NAP identical to website/legal business details.
- Use one primary business entity/name consistently.
- Publish genuine inventory frequently and remove sold stock accurately.
- Ask real customers for honest Google reviews; never fabricate or incentivize misleading reviews.
- Add fresh vehicle photos and local business photos regularly.
- Track `/`, `/ambala`, `/used-cars-ambala`, `/sell`, `/inventory` and published car URLs in Search Console.
- Monitor queries by intent: used cars, second hand cars, car dealer, sell car, vehicle services + Ambala.
- Improve pages from actual query/impression data instead of creating thin keyword pages.
